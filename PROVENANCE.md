# Provenance

The conjecture appears in §5 of Kitamura, Ghosh, Ayoub, Chu and Szepesvári, arXiv:2604.21177v2. The [recovered proof](proof/recovered-construction.md) gives the occupancy-weighted kernel argument. The revision spells out the direct-policy extension and adds an explicit three-state worked example and exact replay.
