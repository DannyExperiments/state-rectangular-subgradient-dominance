# Claim scope

The model has finite state and action sets, fixed costs, stationary randomized direct policies, discount in [0,1), and a full-support initial law. The transition set is a nonempty compact product across states. A whole row may couple its actions and its set may be nonconvex.

The bound holds for every ambient Fréchet subgradient, so it holds also for the smallest Frank–Wolfe gap over such subgradients. The robust objective is evaluated on feasible policies after statewise convexification. The proof uses the original objective’s direct-coordinate subgradients.

Coupled uncertainty across states, uncertain costs, average-reward objectives and other policy parameterizations lie outside the result. The exact finite replay corroborates the value, occupancy and gradient identities. The manuscript gives the proof for arbitrary stated inputs.
