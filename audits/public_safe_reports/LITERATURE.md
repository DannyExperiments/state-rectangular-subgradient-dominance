# Q870 source and prior-result review

**Verdict, 27 November 2026.** High confidence that the result addresses the fixed-cost catalog statement and its minimum over ambient policy subgradients. The literature below contains the statewise Bellman equation, fixed-kernel dominance, earlier restricted guarantees and the explicit general-state-rectangular conjecture. No matching arbitrary compact state-rectangular, fixed-cost, every-subgradient proof appeared in this bounded check. Confidence in that literature verdict is moderate. Priority has not been established.

## Primary question and differential convention

Toshinori Kitamura, Arnob Ghosh, Alex Ayoub, Thang D. Chu and Csaba Szepesvári, *Revisiting Subgradient Dominance in Robust MDPs: Counterexamples, Hardness, and Sufficient Conditions*, arXiv:2604.21177v2, 8 October 2026. The explicit conjecture is in [§5, PDF p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.21177v2#page=10). The [author version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21177v2) lists the initial 23 April version and the October revision. The [conference index](https://neurips.cc/Downloads/2026) lists the work. The October author PDF is the full text used here.

The updated source's [Definition 3.1 and Assumption 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21177v2#S3) state the minimum over Fréchet subgradients followed by the maximum over direct policies, with full-support initial law. The robust model formalism allows joint cost and transition uncertainty. Q870 fixes the costs, restricts the uncertainty to compact statewise transition rows, and retains the exact order of the gap operations. Each state row is an action-indexed tuple and may couple actions. The proof includes zero discount and nonconvex row sets.

[§2.3, Lemma 2.1 and Eqs. (3)–(4)](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21177v2#S2) specify the ambient convex hull of active-model gradients and the direct-coordinate gradient. One can realize these formulas by extending each total cost via the linear policy-coordinate resolvent, with a common open neighborhood where the matrix is invertible, and taking a smooth-branch maximum. The source separately appends the feasible-set indicator when forming the Moreau envelope. The proof uses the ambient maximum over the original models. Convexification of uncertainty is applied to values of feasible policies, and the argument needs no equality of the two ambient extensions. If an outward normal is appended to a subgradient, its pairing contributes nonnegatively to every feasible gap.

## Prior results and limits

- [Kitamura et al., Theorem 4.3 and Proposition 4.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21177v2#S4) establish dominance with a unique worst-case transition kernel or existence of a single worst-case action-value function that pointwise dominates those from all active models. The source covers state-action and its low-rank factor rectangularity through sufficient conditions. [Proposition 5.1 and Appendix F.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21177v2#A6) give one state-rectangular two-state example, whose proof uses a specified initial state, beyond those sufficient conditions. Table 1 and §5 explicitly leave the state-rectangular class open.
- [Wang, Ho and Petrik, ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/wang23i.html) distinguish outer policy optimization from inner kernel optimization. Their Lemma 4.3 concerns the inner kernel-variable gradient at a fixed policy. The general uncertainty policy guarantee in Theorem 3.2 concerns the different proximal-envelope formulation, and [Kitamura et al., §3 and Appendix B.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21177v2#A2) refute it and diagnose the erroneous inequality. Appendix B.2 explains the invalid exchange involving the convex hull of active gradients in Theorem 4 of the earlier Kitamura et al. ICLR 2025 paper. The occupancy-weighted kernel handles an arbitrary convex combination directly.
- [Chen and Huang, ICML 2024, Proposition 3 and Appendix I](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v235/main/assets/chen24s/chen24s.pdf) prove gradient dominance in the kernel variable at fixed policy for a convex compact state-rectangular ambiguity set, including their entropy-regularized cost. The proof invokes Wang et al.'s inner-optimization lemma. Their saddle algorithm gives policy optimization guarantees under its own assumptions. Neither result gives the minimum over outer policy subgradients of the unregularized robust total cost.
- [Kumar, Derman, Geist, Levy and Mannor, NeurIPS 2023, Theorem 5.1](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/ba8aee784ffe0813890288b334444eda-Paper-Conference.pdf) give an explicit robust policy-gradient formula and update costs for their rectangular norm-ball sets. Their §5.1 points to Wang et al. for global convergence. The formula and policy evaluation machinery do not establish dominance for every mixture of tied gradients of arbitrary compact statewise rows.
- [Satheesh, Chen, Huang and Huang, arXiv:2602.11387, §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11387) use entropy-regularized primal/dual objectives and study parameterized uncertainty. Their Lemma 5.3 controls the adversary gradient at fixed policy. [Wang et al., ICML 2025, Theorem 4.4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/wang25ei/wang25ei.pdf) explicitly analyze the adversary's gradient in an average-reward model. These differ both in the optimization variable and, for the latter, in the objective.
- [Wiesemann, Kuhn and Rustem, Math. Oper. Res. 38 (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.1120.0566) establish state-rectangular robust dynamic programming. [Ho, Petrik and Wiesemann, arXiv:2602.05591, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05591) give algorithms for robust Bellman updates. [Agarwal, Kakade, Lee and Mahajan, JMLR 22 (2021)](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v22/19-736.html) give fixed-model policy-gradient dominance. These supply the imported comparison and Bellman ingredients. The key additional identity represents the entire active-gradient convex hull by gradients of active kernels after statewise convexification, using discounted occupancies to change one mixing coefficient per whole row.

Queries covered the exact title and conjecture, the earlier apparent policy-dominance claims, state-rectangular robust Bellman and gradient methods, and recent policy- and kernel-side work. The mathematical conclusion is fixed-cost subgradient dominance. General cost uncertainty, nonrectangular transition ambiguity, average-reward objectives and nonlinear policy parameterizations are outside the result.
