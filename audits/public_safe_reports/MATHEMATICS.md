# Internal mathematical review

The review checks the statewise Bellman contraction, common value for every active kernel, and equality on feasible policies under convexification. It checks the ambient branch gradients at all feasible policies, reweighting whole rows with action-independent coefficients, uniqueness of the occupancy fixed point and the final comparison against a robust minimizer. This comparison is valid for each subgradient, including mixtures at points of nonsmoothness. The proof includes zero discount and policies on the boundary.

External peer review and formal verification are pending.
